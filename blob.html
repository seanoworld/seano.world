RACCOON CITY PUNKS IS OUT!

Dave Scheidt and me both grew up with Resident Evil. We're nerds for that sweet sweet video game stuff. We previously worked on a Castlevania comic and decided to get back together to make Raccoon City Punks, a story about three skater kids that shred through a zombie infected Raccoon City. Some fan favorites of the series show up and are shown in risograph bright red and black ink glory. Dave wrote the story and our good friend Aaron Pittman helped me color the backgrounds. I printed this comic myself using my SF3150 and am currently printing another round of books. It's a wild, stressful, and rewarding experience printing a book like this using riso. I'll have a way for people to buy these soon. This is the biggest book I've printed using Riso and I'm so freaking happy with how it turned out. The Bright Bloody Red is that perfect, shlocky horror movie red. Really makes the SPLOOT sound effects extra SPLOOTY! nyehe
